# Llama Commercial

Commercial cleaning across London: offices and business units, schools and education centres, churches and places of worship, shops, gyms and sports facilities, factories and warehouses, communal areas and car parks. Based in Hackney.

**Tagline:** *No drama. Just Llama.*

## The idea

Llamas are calm, steady and hard-working. That's the feeling a clean should leave with the people who run a building: it was done, it was done properly, and nobody had to chase it. The brand stays calm too. We don't shout offers or stack up guarantees. We say plainly what we clean, how often and who does it.

## Voice

- Short, plain, specific. "The same two cleaners each visit." Never "sparkling", "best in London" or "unbeatable".
- Write what happens: "We walk the building with you", "You get a written specification".
- No exclamation marks, no all-caps sentences, no countdowns or "limited time".
- Free site visits and written quotes are stated once, calmly, near the contact details. Never as a headline.
- Use the pun once per piece, never two in a row.

## Colour

Use roughly 60% surface-page or surface-card, 30% highland, 10% saffron and the rest.

- **Highland** (`highland`, #173d36) carries the brand: the logo tile, header bars, buttons, uniforms and the van.
- **Saffron** (`saffron`, #e3a13a) is the woven band on the llama's neck. Use it for small marks and one highlight per layout. On light backgrounds it is never text; use `saffron-ink` for the tagline.
- Neutrals lean slightly green (`surface-page` #f5f6f2, `surface-sage` #e8ede6). Never cream, never pure grey.
- `ink` for all text, `ink-muted` for secondary text.

**Print:** ask the printer to match the hex values and send a proof. Closest Pantone references for checking: highland ≈ 3435 C, saffron ≈ 7409 C.

### Colour-coded cleaning

The four `code-*` tokens are the standard UK cleaning colour code. They appear only where we explain it (website, staff handbook, cupboard labels), always as a swatch with its word.

| Colour | Area |
| --- | --- |
| Red | Toilets, urinals, washroom floors |
| Blue | General areas: offices, halls, corridors |
| Green | Kitchens, food and bar areas |
| Yellow | Clinical areas, washroom sinks |

## Type

- **Fraunces** 600 for headlines and the wordmark. Sentence case. One display size per layout.
- **Manrope** for everything people read: body 16/26, small 14/20, bold for card titles.
- **IBM Plex Mono** 500, uppercase, tracked, for specification labels, schedules and job references.

All three are free Google Fonts. In Word or Canva, use Georgia for Fraunces and Arial for Manrope if they aren't installed.

## Logo

The mark is an original llama in profile with a woven saffron neck band, inside a highland tile with 22% rounded corners. The wordmark is "Llama" in Fraunces above "COMMERCIAL" in tracked Manrope.

- Clear space on every side: at least the width of the llama's neck (16% of the tile).
- Minimum size: tile 24px on screen or 8mm in print; full lockup 120px or 30mm wide.
- On highland or dark photos, use the white logo (no tile). On light backgrounds, use the green logo (with tile).
- Don't recolour the band, stretch the mark, put the green logo on a busy photo, add shadows or outlines, or redraw the llama.
- Every logo is outlined artwork in `assets/Logos/`: SVG for print and web, 2,000px PNG for Word, Canva and social.

## Layout

- Generous margins (`space-6` on posters, `space-5` between blocks).
- Left-aligned text, one headline, one action.
- Lists and specifications use hairline rules (`line`) instead of boxes.
- Cards use `radius-md`, buttons are pills (`radius-pill`).

## Photography

Use real photos of our own team and sites: people working, finished floors, clean washrooms. No stock photos of smiling models, no rubber gloves, no sparkles. If there's no real photo yet, use a plain highland panel with the mark.
