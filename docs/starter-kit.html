3. Colour code for cloths, mops and buckets

Red: toilets, urinals, washroom floorsBlue: general areasGreen: kitchens and food areasYellow: clinical areas, washroom sinks

Label every bottle, keep red kit in its own bag, and never take a red cloth into a kitchen. Show new staff on their first shift.

4. Set-up checklist

Company and money

  • Run a UK IPO trademark search for "Llama Commercial" (class 37, cleaning services)
  • Register Llama Commercial Ltd at Companies House
  • Open a business bank account
  • Register as an employer with HMRC (PAYE) before the first payday
  • Set up payroll and pension auto-enrolment
  • Pay the ICO data protection fee
  • Choose accounting software and set up the invoice template
  • Check llamacommercial.co.uk is free and register it

Insurance and safety

  • Employers' liability insurance (at least £5m, a legal requirement once you employ staff)
  • Public liability insurance, sized to what your clients ask for
  • COSHH assessment for every chemical, with its safety data sheet in a folder
  • A risk assessment and method statement for each new site
  • Accident book and first aid kit in the van or kit bag
  • Machine training before anyone uses a rotary floor machine

People

  • Right-to-work check for every cleaner before their first shift
  • DBS checks to the level each site needs (schools may ask for more)
  • Written contracts and the staff handbook from the launch pack
  • Basic training: colour code, COSHH, safe lifting, using the machines
  • Consider BICSc Licence to Practice for lead cleaners (assessment slot £50)
  • Uniforms ordered with the logo

Selling

  • Business mobile number and WhatsApp Business set up
  • Website live with your real number and email
  • Google Business Profile with the social avatar and real photos
  • Business cards and flyers printed (print files in the brand pack)
  • Quote calculator and negotiation desk bookmarked on your phone
  • Site specification, quote and visit report templates ready
  • Key holding log and alarm code record
  • A list of the first 50 buildings to visit: offices, schools, churches, shops near Hackney
