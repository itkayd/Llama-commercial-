About us

A London cleaning company built around the building.

Llama Commercial cleans offices, schools, churches, shops, gyms, factories, communal areas and car parks across London. We're based in Hackney and work across Greater London.

Every contract starts with a site visit and a written specification. That way you and our team know exactly what gets cleaned, how often and to what standard. We send the same cleaners each visit so they get to know your building.

Everyone who joins us goes through right-to-work and reference checks, and DBS checks where a site needs them. They're trained in safe chemical handling, colour-coded cleaning and the machines they use.

We use professional equipment and choose lower-impact products wherever they do the job properly. We work in and outside business hours, including early mornings, evenings and weekends.

We hold public liability and employers' liability insurance, and we're happy to send certificates before we start.

Floors & carpets

Carpet & upholstery cleaning

Office carpets, pubs, halls, chairs, sofas and fabric partitions.

Every 6 to 12 months · after spills

Rug cleaning

Reception rugs, church rugs and runners.

One-off · seasonal

Hard floor care

Vinyl, safety flooring, linoleum, sealed wood, stone and terrazzo.

Scrub monthly · strip and seal once or twice a year

  • Scrubbing: machine scrubbing to lift ground-in dirt
  • Stripping: old, scuffed polish removed down to the floor
  • Sealing: fresh coats of seal or polish to protect the surface
  • Polishing: buffing to bring back the shine between treatments
