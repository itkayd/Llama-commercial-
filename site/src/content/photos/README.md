# Photos

Add real photos of the team and real sites here (`.jpg`, `.png`, `.webp` or `.avif`).
`PhotoStrip` shows them automatically; while this folder has no images it renders nothing.

- Real team and real sites only. No stock photos or AI images.
- Natural light, people working. No posed smiles, rubber-glove clichés, sparkles or lens flares.
- Name files descriptively, e.g. `scrubbing-a-school-corridor.jpg`. The name becomes the alt text.
- Get permission from anyone pictured and from the site owner.
